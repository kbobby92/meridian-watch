# Meridian Watch

A geopolitical situation monitor: a 3D globe of world hotspots with risk dossiers, a commodities watchlist, chokepoint oil flows, a sanctions tracker, conflict timelines, and live air and sea traffic.

- **Live aircraft** load in the browser as one worldwide snapshot from [adsb.lol](https://adsb.lol) or [adsb.fi](https://adsb.fi), directly or through public CORS relays, refreshed every 30 seconds. If none answers, the page sweeps the area you're looking at plus every hotspot and chokepoint via adsb.lol or [airplanes.live](https://airplanes.live). Military aircraft are highlighted.
- **Live vessels** stream from [AISStream.io](https://aisstream.io) for the Gulf, Red Sea, Eastern Mediterranean, Black Sea, Baltic, Taiwan Strait, Malacca, the Cape and Panama.
- **Briefing data** (`data.json`: hotspots, news, markets, trade measures, timelines) is refreshed twice a day, at 7:30 AM and 6 PM Arizona time.

Risk scores are an analytical assessment of public reporting, not an official index. Prices are snapshots from public market reports and are not for trading.
